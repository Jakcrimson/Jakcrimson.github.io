A proposal for a **World Artificial Intelligence Regulation Office** — a
single, binding global authority for AI, conceived as an impartial
multidisciplinary consortium of scientists, legal experts and economists rather
than an "AI police".

The paper sets out a theoretical structure for such an office — Science and
Technology, Legal and Ethics, Economics and Societal Impact, and Ecological
Sustainability departments — then works through the parts that are usually left
vague: who pays for it, who governs it, and how a global standard could be
enforced without overriding national sovereignty. The proposed route is a
scaffolding of soft power, market access incentives and domestic ratification,
reached over three phases from a coalition of the willing to full operational
capacity.

It also reviews what already exists — the UN High-level Advisory Body, GPAI,
the OECD AI Principles, the UNESCO Recommendation, ISO standards — and argues
that the missing component is not principles but a mechanism to make them
binding.

Written in August 2025, during the MSc. A follow-up written a year later is
[*A Moment to Talk About AI*]({{ '/posts/a-moment-to-talk-about-ai/' | relative_url }}).
